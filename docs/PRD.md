# Product Requirements Document (PRD)
## University & College Management System

### 1. Product Overview
The University & College Management System is a centralized platform designed to help a university manage and oversee its colleges while enabling each college to manage its academic and administrative operations, departments, students, and staff.

The platform provides digital services for university administrators, college administrators, faculty members, teaching assistants, students, and administrative staff through one integrated system.

### 2. Problem & Opportunity
Although the university and its colleges already have websites and some digital services, there is an opportunity to improve management, coordination, and service delivery through a unified platform connecting different university stakeholders.

Key opportunities include:
- Providing centralized university-level oversight of colleges and their operations.
- Enabling each college to manage its academic and administrative activities.
- Establishing an official communication channel for announcements and notifications, reducing reliance on WhatsApp and Facebook posts.
- Providing personalized student timetables based on registered courses, including courses from different academic years.
- Connecting academic, educational, administrative, and financial services in one platform.
- Improving attendance tracking, course registration, and learning management.

### 3. Product Vision
To create an integrated university platform that enables the university to manage its colleges efficiently, empowers each college to manage its academic and administrative operations, and provides students, faculty members, and employees with a unified digital experience.

### 4. Product Goals
- Enable university administration to oversee and manage its colleges through one platform.
- Enable college administration to manage departments and academic and administrative operations.
- Simplify services and procedures for students and alumni.
- Improve attendance management, academic scheduling, and course registration.
- Provide a digital learning environment for sharing materials, assignments, quizzes, and academic communication.
- Establish an official communication channel between university administration, colleges, and their members.
- Organize the management and tracking of university fees and payments.

### 5. Target Users & Their Needs

**University Administration**

*University President*
- Oversee the university and its colleges.
- Monitor overall university operations and performance.
- Access university-wide reports and information.

*University Vice President*
- Support university leadership and oversee assigned university sectors.
- Monitor activities and performance within their areas of responsibility.

*University Secretary-General*
- Oversee the university's administrative operations.
- Follow up on general administrative affairs across the university.

**College Administration**

*Dean*
- Lead the college and oversee its academic and administrative operations.
- Monitor the performance of departments and administrative units.
- Follow up on the overall educational process within the college.

*Vice Dean for Education and Student Affairs*
- Oversee educational activities and student affairs.
- Follow up on course registration, academic schedules, and student-related academic procedures.

*Vice Dean for Postgraduate Studies and Research*
- Oversee postgraduate programs and research activities.
- Follow up on postgraduate academic affairs and related activities.

*Vice Dean for Community Service and Environmental Development*
- Oversee community service activities.
- Follow up on initiatives and activities related to environmental development.

*Faculty Secretary*
- Support the dean's office in managing the college's administrative affairs.
- Follow up on administrative work and coordination within the college.

**Student Affairs**

*Student Affairs Manager*
- Oversee student records and student affairs operations.
- Supervise registration procedures and student-related services.
- Monitor students' academic status and administrative requests.

*Student Affairs Staff*
- Update and maintain student records.
- Handle course registration procedures and student requests.
- Assist students with academic and administrative services.

**Alumni Affairs**

*Alumni Affairs Manager*
- Oversee alumni records and services.
- Supervise requests for graduation certificates and official documents.

*Alumni Affairs Staff*
- Maintain alumni information and records.
- Process requests for certificates and other graduation-related documents.

**Financial Affairs**

*Financial Manager*
- Oversee the college's financial operations.
- Monitor student fees, payments, outstanding balances, and financial records.

*Financial Staff*
- Handle student fees and payment records.
- Assist with financial procedures and payment-related services.

**Academic Departments**

*Department Head*
- Lead the academic department.
- Oversee department courses, faculty members, and academic activities.
- Follow up on department schedules and educational operations.

**Academic Staff**

*Faculty Member*
- Manage assigned courses and educational materials.
- Record student attendance and grades.
- Manage assignments and assessments.
- Communicate with students through the platform.

*Teaching Assistant*
- Manage tutorial groups and laboratory sessions.
- Record attendance and follow up on practical educational activities.
- Support students and assist with assigned courses.

**Students**

*Student*
- Access courses, learning materials, and announcements.
- Register for courses and follow academic status.
- View personalized timetables based on registered courses, including courses from different academic years.
- Track attendance, grades, and payments.
- Submit requests and access university services.

### 6. Value Proposition
The platform delivers value to different university stakeholders:

- **University Administration:** Centralized oversight and improved visibility into college operations.
- **College Administration:** More organized management of academic and administrative activities.
- **Administrative Staff:** Easier management and tracking of responsibilities and services.
- **Academic Departments:** Better coordination of courses, teaching staff, and department activities.
- **Faculty Members and Teaching Assistants:** Unified tools for managing educational activities and communicating with students.
- **Students and Alumni:** Convenient access to university information, services, and procedures through one platform.

### 7. Success Metrics
- University administration's ability to oversee colleges through the platform.
- Adoption of the system by colleges for academic and administrative management.
- Students' ability to access personalized timetables that accommodate their registered courses.
- Adoption of the platform as an official communication and announcements channel.
- Improved organization and tracking of student attendance.
- Adoption of learning management tools by faculty members and students.
- Improved efficiency of course registration and payment tracking.
